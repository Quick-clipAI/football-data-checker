Football data checker

See what your API-Football plan really covers: how many leagues, which data types, which seasons, and how many requests are left today. It goes through your own site, so no key is typed here.

A. One match in full: PSG v Barcelona

Finds their match, then asks for line-ups, full statistics, events and player ratings, one request at a time, and reads your quota before and after each so you see exactly what every piece costs. About 7 requests in total (1 to find the match, 6 for the pieces). The cost of each piece is read from API-Football's own requests-left counter that comes back with every answer, so no extra requests are spent measuring.

B. Players, transfers and injuries (Manchester United)

Tests what the plan returns for the data we want for player cards and availability: a club's transfers (with dates and loan/free/fee type), the injuries list, the current squad, one player's sidelined history, and the coach. 6 requests. Each row shows what came back or the plan's refusal, so we know what we can build on.
